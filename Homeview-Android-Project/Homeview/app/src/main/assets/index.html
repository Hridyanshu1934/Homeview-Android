Connect your Echo Dot
Pair it as a Bluetooth speaker for tablet audio.

1. Say “Alexa, pair Bluetooth.”
2. On Android, open Bluetooth settings.
3. Select your Echo Dot from available devices.
4. Return to Homeview and play audio.

Alexa app ↗
Alexa voice commands still work directly through the Echo Dot. This first version does not remotely invoke Alexa routines from the dashboard.
